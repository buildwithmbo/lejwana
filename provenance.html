Services · Provenance

Records around the goods.

Assessed stone by stone, shared where the record allows.

What exists

Provenance is assessed stone by stone, or parcel by parcel. What is available varies with the goods and the source: GIA certification, laser inscription, Kimberley Process certification, rough imagery, cutting and polishing material, and transaction records.

The digital journey

Where the record allows, this is consolidated into a digital journey a jeweller can pass to an end client, viewable and traceable wherever they are.

The honest limit

Completeness depends on what was captured upstream. The journey is offered stone by stone, never as a blanket feature of every diamond.
