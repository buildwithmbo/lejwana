What is a Sight?

Lejwana · Trade explainer

How De Beers’ scheduled rough sales work.

A Sight is one of De Beers’ scheduled rough-diamond sales. Instead of auctioning stones, De Beers sells to a defined group of customers, known as Sightholders, on a fixed calendar through the year.

Each Sightholder is allocated a box of rough in the assortments it has agreed to buy. The buyer inspects the goods, then decides whether to take them at the price offered. Prices are set by De Beers rather than discovered through bidding.

Because the calendar is public, Sights are useful signposts. They tell the market when a large share of rough supply is changing hands, and what buyers do with it afterwards often shows in secondary-market premiums and discounts.

Access is by contract. If you are not a Sightholder, the stones you see have usually passed through a Sightholder or another sales channel first.
