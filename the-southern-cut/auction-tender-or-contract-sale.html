Auction, tender or contract sale?

Lejwana · Trade explainer

The main ways rough reaches buyers.

Rough diamonds reach manufacturers through three main routes.

Contract sales are agreed in advance between a producer and a registered customer. Volume, assortment and timing are set by the agreement, and pricing follows the producer’s list. This gives the buyer a steady supply and the seller a predictable outlet. The Sight system is the best-known example.

Auctions are open competitive sales. Registered buyers inspect the lots, then bid, and the highest bid wins. Spot auctions, such as those run by the Okavango Diamond Company, price the goods as the market sees them on the day.

Tenders are sealed-bid sales. Buyers view the goods, then submit one confidential price per lot. The seller awards each lot to the best bid. Tenders are common for larger or exceptional stones, where a single price matters more than a rolling set of bids.

Each route suits different goods and different buyers. Most producers use more than one.
