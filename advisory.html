Services · Advisory

Navigate the Diamond Pipeline

We provide expert guidance at every critical stage, from accurate rough diamond assessment and realistic yield forecasting to verified provenance and traceability.

From rough to polished

Lejwana understands the decisions that shape a diamond before it reaches the polished market: how rough is assessed and sorted, what it can produce once cut, and how that affects the value of the finished stone.

Rough-to-Polished Yield Modelling

We assess run-of-mine parcels and individual stones through crystal morphology, internal strain analysis and inclusion mapping. By modelling optimal cutting strategies, from laser sawing and bruting to final polishing, we forecast realistic weight retention and polished diamond recovery before you commit to a purchase.

Pricing & Rapaport Benchmarking

We provide market-led valuations that go beyond the basic 4Cs. Using current Rapaport price lists and real-world trade discounts, we assess polished diamonds based on cut quality, 3EX proportions, fluorescence, BGM (brown, green and milky) characteristics and carat-weight price thresholds. This gives you a clearer picture of market value, achievable wholesale pricing and commercial liquidity.

Beneficiation, Kimberley Process & Provenance

We help navigate Southern Africa’s regulatory and compliance requirements, from Kimberley Process (KP) certification and government valuation procedures to local beneficiation obligations. We review chain-of-custody documentation and digital provenance records to support transparent, traceable and compliant exports and wholesale transactions.

Beneficiation & Downstream Operations

We provide commercial and technical guidance for establishing and developing local diamond cutting and polishing operations. From rough parcel selection and factory workflow to production planning, yield targets and manufacturing costs per carat, we help downstream ventures assess commercial feasibility and build a path towards sustainable production.

Assorting, Grading & Bench Training

We deliver practical technical training tailored to sorting offices, cutting factories and diamond trade teams. Training covers rough diamond assorting and sieve sizing, 10x loupe grading, blocking and brillianteering standards, and commercial parcel valuation. The focus is on developing practical skills, improving consistency and strengthening commercial decision-making.

How we work: on retainer or by project, independent of any purchase through Lejwana.
