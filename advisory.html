What we advise on

Rough-to-polished economics

A parcel of rough is a set of decisions: how to plan each stone, what to cut, what to recover and what the polished result will fetch. We model those decisions before money moves. That means yield estimates grounded in polishing experience, cutting plans weighed against the polished market, and a clear view of where value is created or lost between mine and sale.

The work suits producers deciding how to sell, manufacturers deciding what to buy, and investors who need the transformation from rough to polished explained in numbers they can test.

Southern African market intelligence

The house operates in Botswana, Angola, South Africa and Namibia. That position produces intelligence that cannot be assembled at a distance: how each national framework is applied in practice, where supply is developing, which corridors are open and what goods are actually achieving. We brief clients on the region as it is, not as it is reported.
