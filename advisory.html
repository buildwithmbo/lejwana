From rough to polished

Lejwana understands the decisions that shape a diamond before it reaches the polished market: how rough is assessed and sorted, what it can produce once cut, and how that affects the value of the finished stone.

Rough to polished

How rough is sorted and valued; what it may yield through planning, sawing and polishing; and how cut, make, size and quality affect the finished diamond.

Pricing and valuation

How a polished diamond compares with the market, where it sits against the relevant list, and how shape, colour, clarity, size and make influence its price.

Policy, clearance and provenance

How beneficiation policy, export procedures and trade practices differ across producing countries, and what provenance records may be available with polished goods.

Industrial development

Practical commercial insight for projects that aim to build more value locally—from sorting and valuation to cutting, polishing and jewellery manufacture.

Skills and training

Hands-on industry knowledge across rough evaluation, polished grading, cutting and polishing, and the commercial side of the diamond trade. Training and technical input can be shaped around the needs of a business, project or team.
