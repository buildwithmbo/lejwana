Southern African Polished Forum 2026, Gaborone

Lejwana · 14–16 October 2026

When
14–16 October 2026
Where
Gaborone, Botswana
Who it is for
Producers, manufacturers, polished buyers and trade advisers
Lejwana
Attending all three days

Three days in Gaborone on what the region does with its own production: beneficiation policy, the state of local manufacturing capacity, and how polished goods from Southern Africa reach buyers abroad. The programme runs sessions on rough allocation, cutting and polishing capacity, and the documentation buyers outside the region now expect to see with a stone.

Why we are going

Most of the region's business is still done face to face, and a forum of this kind puts producers, manufacturers and buyers in the same building for three days. For a house that brokers between them, that is worth more than the sessions themselves.

We will be listening for two things in particular: what local manufacturers are seeing in yields this year, and how buyers outside the region are treating provenance documentation now that more of them ask for it as standard.

Meeting us there

We keep time free around the sessions for conversations with producers and buyers. If you are attending and want to sit down, tell us before the week begins and we will hold a slot.
