About

Lejwana

Lejwana brokers, deals and advises on natural polished diamonds across Southern Africa. We believe every diamond tells a story, and as a Southern African company, we are proud to share the brilliance of our heritage with the world. Our vision is to connect customers to the beauty, authenticity, and integrity of Africa’s diamonds, ensuring every gem reflects the values of trust, equity, and excellence.

Mission & Approach

Mission

We are committed to making every experience unique and meaningful. Whether sourcing ethically mined diamonds, crafting bespoke jewellery, or forging partnerships, we approach every interaction with honesty, care, and precision

How We Do It

We honour our roots by upholding the highest standards of ethical sourcing, embracing innovation in our craftsmanship, and fostering collaboration in every relationship. By treating each deal and customer with individual attention, we ensure our work reflects our values of trustworthiness and ingenuity.

Our Promise

We empower our communities, share our success, and celebrate the beauty of diversity. At Lejwana, we don’t just deliver diamonds—we create enduring connections and unforgettable experiences that inspire confidence and value in every customer and partner we serve.

Our Values

Authenticity & Integrity
As a native Botswana company, we uphold the highest standards of honesty and transparency, offering ethically sourced diamonds fully traceable to our local trading house and mines and our nation’s rich heritage.
Community & Culture
Our business thrives on the confluence of diverse people—customers, colleagues, and communities—united by shared values and a deep cultural connection.
Trust & Reliability
We deliver on our promises with consistency and faithfulness, building enduring partnerships through dependable and mutually rewarding relationships.
Excellence & Ingenuity
We aim for exceptional results in every endeavour, leveraging smart, efficient, and innovative solutions to exceed expectations.
Equity & Equality
We are committed to empowering communities by sharing wealth, supporting youth, and fostering opportunities that promote fairness and inclusion

Our Team

Lejwana brings together diamond specialists, financial professionals and industry experts with deep experience across Botswana and the wider Southern African diamond industry.

Our team combines technical expertise with local knowledge, international standards and a network built over many years across the diamond value chain. With Botswana-born professionals at the heart of our team, we bring a perspective grounded in the region and connected to the global market.

Our collective expertise spans diamond valuation, polishing, gemmology, sourcing, manufacturing, finance, governance, risk and compliance. Supported by professional qualifications and industry affiliations, we bring both technical rigour and commercial understanding to every engagement.

As the diamond industry evolves, we are helping shape what comes next. We are deliberately low profile, relationship-led and focused on creating greater transparency, efficiency and value across the industry.
