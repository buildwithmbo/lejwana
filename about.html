About

A Southern African polished diamond house.

Lejwana brokers, deals and advises on natural polished diamonds across Botswana, Angola, South Africa and Namibia. Founded in Gaborone. Members of the London Diamond Bourse and the World Federation of Diamond Bourses.

Who we are

Lejwana is a Southern African house. It was founded in Gaborone, operates in each of the region's producing countries, and works in natural diamonds only. The operating model is regional: local presence in each hub, direct relationships with cutting and polishing works, and goods assessed where they are, by the house's own people.

Source-country integration is built into how the business runs. The house does not extract value from the region and move it elsewhere. It works inside each country's own diamond framework, so that every transaction supports the market it came from.

Where we stand

The house's position is straightforward. Provenance is not a marketing line but a documented fact, carried by every stone. Natural diamonds are the only goods traded, without exception. And the source country's strategy comes first: sourcing, cutting, placing and partnership should strengthen the country the stone came from, not bypass it.

What the house holds to

Provenance

Every stone the house handles carries a GIA report, laser inscription, country-of-origin paperwork and iTraceiT blockchain verification. Provenance is documented, not asserted.

Natural only

The house trades natural diamonds exclusively. No laboratory-grown goods are handled, in any capacity, under any arrangement.

The source country

The house works within each country's national diamond framework, from Botswana's beneficiation policy to South Africa's transformation requirements.
