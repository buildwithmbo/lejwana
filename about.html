About

Lejwana

Lejwana is a Southern African company. We broker, trade and advise on natural polished diamonds sourced across Botswana, Angola, South Africa and Namibia, from a base in Gaborone and membership of the London Diamond Bourse. Lejwana works for buyers who want reliable regional access, careful screening, and a transaction that is documented and discreet.

Our Core Values

Integrity

We trade what we can document, and represent a stone as it is, not as it might be sold.

Relationships

The business runs on direct, repeated relationships: with the region's factories on one side and trade buyers on the other.

Probity

Every transaction is carried under a documented process, and under London Diamond Bourse and WFDB conduct.

What Lejwana Stands On

Access
Lejwana gives buyers access to selected regional factories and their available polished stock. Stones are sourced to a brief and screened before they are put forward, so what reaches you is filtered rather than listed.
Standards
Natural polished diamonds only. Lejwana trades what it can document. Every stone is GIA graded, inscriptions are checked where they apply, suppliers are screened, and what a stone is and is not gets stated plainly.
Process
One accountable process carries the stone from source to buyer: selection, documentation, insured shipment and settlement. You receive the stone that was described, with the paperwork to support it.

Meet Our Team

Lejwana is run by four directors.

Thoko Tshuma(ACCA, MBA)

Managing Director

Thoko is responsible for Lejwana’s commercial direction and delivery across sourcing, trading and advisory. He develops the counterparty relationships the business depends on, defines the mandates Lejwana takes on, and oversees transactions from first enquiry through to settlement. A certified diamond polisher and valuator, and a member of the London Diamond Bourse, he decides what is put in front of a buyer, on what terms, and stands behind it.

Onneile Maripe(BFP, FCA)

Finance, Governance and Risk Director

A Chartered Accountant with diamond-industry experience at Debswana Diamond Company, and an Independent Trustee of the Stanbic Bank Botswana Pension Fund, Onneile is responsible for financial control, governance and risk at Lejwana. She reviews commercial terms, records, settlement arrangements and counterparty considerations, supporting transaction prudence, due diligence and financial discipline. Her role helps protect transaction integrity and gives clients assurance that commitments are approached with proper scrutiny.

Paresh Kumar

Technical Director

A fourth-generation Surat diamantaire with manufacturing experience across Surat, Kimberley and Southern Africa, Paresh is responsible for valuation, grading and quality assurance at Lejwana. He physically inspects goods against the 4Cs and the wider considerations of make, matching, proportions and presentation. The aim is to ensure that what is put forward is the strongest available fit for the specification, not simply the easiest to obtain, and that any shortfall is stated plainly.

Baone Sefhemo

Operations Director

Drawing on operations experience at Remnant Diamonds Botswana, Baone is responsible for documentation, timing and the agreed movement of goods at Lejwana. He coordinates clearance, insured transit and handovers, keeping records aligned between the relevant parties and planning discretion into each movement. His role helps ensure that goods move in accordance with the agreed arrangements and that the supporting paperwork is in order.
