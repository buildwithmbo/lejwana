About

Regional polished diamonds. Clearer trade access.

We source, broker and trade natural polished diamonds across Botswana, Angola, South Africa, Namibia and Lesotho.

Based in Gaborone, we work with established jewellers, dealers, manufacturers and sourcing partners who want relevant regional stock, technically sound selection and a controlled transaction.

We work to a defined brief: sourcing through selected regional factories and suppliers, reviewing the stones put forward, and running the commercial process through to delivery.

How a mandate moves

  1. 1. Brief

    You set the requirement: shape, carat weight, colour, clarity, cut, quantity, budget, destination and timing.

  2. 2. Source

    We approach selected regional factories and suppliers for available polished stock that meets, or closely meets, the brief.

  3. 3. Review

    We assess proposed stones against the requirement, the grading information and available supporting documentation.

  4. 4. Agree

    Price, payment, documentation, delivery method and commercial terms are settled before goods move.

  5. 5. Deliver

    We manage the documented, insured movement of goods through clearance, handover and settlement.

How we operate

Straight representation

We represent a stone as it is, not as it might be sold. If a stone does not meet the brief, if documentation is limited, or if there is a compromise to consider, we make that clear before a buyer decides.

Direct relationships

We work through direct, repeated relationships with selected regional factories, suppliers and trade buyers. That gives buyers a better-informed route to available stock than an open marketplace can.

Disciplined execution

Technical assessment, commercial terms, documentation, financial review and logistics are handled as one connected transaction. Selecting the stone is not the finish. The mandate closes when the stone, the records, the payment and the handover are all in order.
