How brokerage works

Sourcing

You brief us on the goods you need: shape, size, colour, clarity, quantity and budget. We take that brief into the region, working through polishing works and dealers in Botswana, Angola, South Africa and Namibia. Because we operate in each hub, we see goods before they travel and before they are marked up along the chain.

Matching

Goods that fit the brief are assessed against it, stone by stone. Where a parcel is close but not exact, we tell you where it differs and what that means for price. You decide with the full picture, not a summary written to close a sale.

Facilitation

We attend the sale on your behalf, negotiate under your instruction and manage the transaction through to settlement. Clearing, insurance and export documentation are arranged by us, so goods move from seller to you under one accountable process.

Consignment and memo

For established counterparties, goods can move on consignment or memo terms. We agree the terms with the supplier, document the parcel in full before it travels, and manage the return or settlement at the end of the period.

Traceability

Every brokered stone carries its provenance with it:

  • Origin and mine
  • Laser inscription
  • ORIGIN/Tracr reference
  • Chain of custody

Where a brief calls for specific cutting or manufacture, we coordinate the work with partner polishing works in the region, under the same documentation standard.
