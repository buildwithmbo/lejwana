What Lejwana does

Local presence and expertise

Lejwana has a local presence in each Southern African diamond hub, with a network built on direct relationships with cutting and polishing works. The team is founded and run by a certified diamond polisher and valuator, and brings industrial, technical and commercial knowledge to every parcel.

Buyers reach the right goods, correctly assessed, without needing their own presence in the region.

Clearing and logistics

Lejwana arranges secure shipping, insurance, customs clearance and Kimberley Process documentation. Goods move under bonded, insured transport with an unbroken chain of custody. Each stone ships with a GIA report, laser inscription, country-of-origin paperwork and iTraceiT blockchain verification.

Buyers receive the exact goods they bought, fully documented and provenance-verified.

Advisory and market access

Lejwana advises on rough-to-polished optimisation, polished value, supply-chain structure, and consumer and trade demand. London Diamond Bourse membership connects Southern African supply directly to the UK and European trade under the bourse's terms of trade.

Buyers and producers reach the right market with goods priced and positioned to sell.
