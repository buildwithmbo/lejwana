The trading proposition

What we trade

Natural polished diamonds only, cut from rough mined in Botswana, Angola, South Africa and Namibia. No laboratory-grown goods, no treated stones, no mixed parcels of uncertain origin. The house trades what it can document, and nothing else.

How stones are documented

Every stone is GIA graded and laser inscribed. Country-of-origin paperwork travels with the goods, with Kimberley Process certification and iTraceiT verification where they apply.

How we sell

Goods are placed by enquiry rather than through a public list. Tell us what you are looking for and the house responds with what it holds or can source, priced against the current market. Trade buyers can leave a standing brief, so suitable goods are offered as they come in.
