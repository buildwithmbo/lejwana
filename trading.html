Services · Trading

Certified natural polished diamonds.

Supplied through local trade relationships, against your requirement.

The goods

Natural polished only, cut from rough mined in Botswana, Angola, South Africa and Namibia. Singles, matched pairs, parcels and layouts, with particular strength in fancy colour. No laboratory-grown, no treated stones.

Documentation

GIA certification and laser inscription, with Kimberley Process paperwork and country-of-origin records travelling with the goods where they exist.

How goods move

By enquiry rather than a public list. Tell us the requirement and we come back with what is available and where it sits against the list. Standing requirements are kept on file and goods offered as they come in.
