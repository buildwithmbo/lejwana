# Adding an event or an article

For the Lejwana team. You can edit events and articles. The other pages are
looked after by the developer.

Log in at **lejwana.com/wp-login.php** with the Editor account you were given.

## Add an event

Events appear in **What's on** on The Southern Cut, and the next eight upcoming
ones run in the "In the trade" bar on the homepage.

1. In the left menu choose **Events**, then **Add event**.
2. **Title:** the name as it should read, for example "De Beers Sight 9".
3. In **Event details**, fill in:
   - **Start date.** Leave **End date** empty for a single day.
   - **Type:** Sale, Tender, Result or Event.
   - **Link URL:** the organiser's page, starting `https://`.
   - **Place:** for example "Gaborone, Botswana".
   - **Status note:** for example "Confirmed".
4. Tick the **Countries** it relates to. Leave all empty for an event that
   belongs to none of the five countries; it will still show under "All countries".
5. Press **Publish**.

The site works out "In 6 days", "Live · day 2 of 4" and "Ended" by itself. Ended
events move to the bottom of the list and leave the homepage bar. Nothing to
tidy up. To remove an event early, open it and choose **Move to Trash**.

## Add an article

1. In the left menu choose **Posts**, then **Add New**.
2. Type the **title**, then write in the body. Use only paragraphs, headings,
   lists and quotes. For headings, pick **Heading 2** or **Heading 3**.
3. In the right-hand panel, under **Post**:
   - **Categories:** tick one. Use **Explainers** only for trade explainers.
   - **Excerpt:** one or two sentences. This is the summary shown on the hub,
     on Insights and in the list of related reads.
4. Optional: **Search results text** below the body is the title and description
   Google shows. Leave it empty to use the article title and excerpt.
5. Check the preview, then **Publish**. The date shown on the article is the
   publish date, so use **Publish: Immediately** only on the day.

New articles appear on Insights and in "Latest intelligence" automatically.
Each article shows two related reads from the same category.

## Before you publish

Everything you publish is live at once and nobody else checks the wording.
Read it through, check the names, figures and dates, and use **Save draft**
until you are happy.

## If something looks wrong

Do not edit the other pages or the site settings. Email the developer with the
address of the page and what you expected to see.
