The trading proposition

What we trade

Natural polished diamonds only, cut from rough mined in Botswana, Angola, South Africa and Namibia. No laboratory-grown goods, no treated stones, no mixed parcels of uncertain origin. The house trades what it can document, and nothing else.

How stones are documented

Every stone is GIA graded and laser inscribed. Country-of-origin paperwork travels with the goods, alongside Kimberley Process certification and iTraceiT blockchain verification. A buyer can trace the stone they hold back to the country, and where the record allows, the mine, it came from.

How we sell

Goods are placed by enquiry rather than through a public list. Tell us what you are looking for and we will respond with what the house holds or can source, priced against the current market. Trade counterparties can also register a standing brief, so relevant goods are offered as they arrive.
