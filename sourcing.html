Services · Sourcing

Sourced to your specification.

Send the specification. We work the network, view the goods, and report back with what fits.

The specification

Shape, weight, colour, clarity, cut, fluorescence, certification, budget, timing and any provenance requirement. Fancy colour and matched layouts are handled on the same basis.

Working the network

We go to the cutting and polishing partners and dealers we hold relationships with across Botswana, Angola, South Africa and Namibia. Goods are viewed on the ground before anything is put to you.

What comes back

What fits the specification, what it prices at against the list, and what certification and provenance records sit behind it. Where a parcel is close but not exact, we say where it differs and what that means for price.

What to send

The more of the specification we have, the more precise the first response.

Specification Parameter Details / Options
Shape Round brilliant, princess, oval, pear, emerald, cushion, marquise
Weight Carat range, or a total weight for a parcel or layout
Colour D to Z, or fancy colour where required
Clarity FL to I, with any inclusion preference
Cut, polish, symmetry Including triple excellent where specified
Fluorescence None to strong
Certification GIA preferred; state any alternative accepted
Commercial Budget or discount off the list, quantity and timing
Provenance Origin preference and the records required
